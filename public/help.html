Help Center

Everything you need to know about managing your streamer groups.

🚀 Getting Started

Signing In

Simply click the Login with Twitch button. We use OAuth2 to securely verify your identity without ever seeing your password.

Tiers

Free Groups must include at least one Washodo core member.

Paid Full customizability for your groups.

👥 Group Management

Creating Groups

Use the Create New Group button. Enter a name and a comma-separated list of Twitch usernames.

Status Messages

You can set a custom message (e.g., "The gang is all here!") that appears at the top of your OBS widgets.

🖼️ Widget Setup

OBS Browser Source

1. Copy your unique Widget URL from the dashboard.
2. In OBS, add a Browser Source.
3. Paste the URL and set your preferred dimensions.

Web Embeds

Widgets are responsive and can be linked in Twitch "About" panels or social bios.

🔔 Notifications

Webhooks

We support Discord, Slack, and Microsoft Teams. Just paste your webhook URL into the group's notification settings.

Custom Payloads

Use variables like {{streamer}}, {{status}}, and {{url}} to customize how notifications appear in your chat.

❓ FAQ

Update Frequency

Statuses are updated every 5 minutes via a background worker.

Privacy

We only store public Twitch data. Engagement metrics are aggregated and anonymized.
